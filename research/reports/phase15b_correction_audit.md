## 2. Model Lock Decision: Outcome B Certified

Following forensic audit of all evidence, the model lock decision is officially classified under:

$$\mathbf{OUTCOME\ B:\ F2\ REMAINS\ FINAL\ MODEL\ WITH\ QUALIFIED\ MULTI\text{-}CRITERIA\ JUSTIFICATION}$$

### Scientific Rationale:
1. **Validation Rule Compliance:** Zero proposed candidates qualified during Stage 15B-S screening. F2 remains the locked production model by protocol default.
2. **Cross-Dataset Performance Balance:** F2 achieves the lowest 5-seed mean MAE on PJM (250.97 MW) and UCI (7.74 MW), and second-lowest on GEFCom (12.41 kW vs 12.36 kW for Control B). It provides the best overall balance across utility, regional, and customer scales.
3. **Architectural Parsimony:** Removing dynamic confidence (Control B) yields a statistically non-significant difference on GEFCom while slightly degrading PJM (+2.53 MW). Control B does not demonstrate clear general superiority.
4. **Transparent Limitation Acknowledgment:** F2 is explicitly acknowledged as not being universally optimal on every dataset (Control B achieves lower mean MAE on GEFCom).
