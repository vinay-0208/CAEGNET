## 1. Executive Summary

This comprehensive audit investigates and reconciles all methodological, architectural, and statistical inconsistencies discovered during the development of CAEG-Net versions V1, V2, and V3. 

The core mission of the CAEG-Net project is:
> *"To determine whether context-aware adaptive expert gating can dynamically route between complementary temporal inductive biases (LSTM, TCN, and CNN) to outperform static ensembling and standalone deep architectures in short-term electricity load forecasting."*

During exploratory research cycles in Phases 2 through 7:
1. The **expert family was substituted** with GRU, Causal TCN, and PatchTemporal backbones, diverging from the canonical LSTM + TCN + CNN architecture.
2. The **recent baseline forecast error** context feature was questioned regarding causality and target leakage.
3. The **statistical evaluation protocol** suffered from overlapping-horizon autocorrelation pathologies and unadjusted multi-step test statistics.
4. **Test-set feedback loops** emerged where models were tuned against historical test evaluations rather than strictly within validation partitions.

This audit establishes the rigorous evidentiary basis for resolving each issue, restores the canonical model boundaries, and sets the standard for all Phase 5 experimental validations.

## 2. Identified Methodological Inconsistencies & Resolutions

### 2.1 Expert Family Integrity: The V2/V3 Deviation
* **Identified Deviation**: During Phase 2–4 development, the original expert family (LSTM, TCN, CNN) was replaced by GRU, Causal TCN, and PatchTemporal/PatchLinear. While this yielded lower raw point-forecast errors (MAE ~239 MW vs ~251 MW), it altered the core hypothesis. Furthermore, standalone CNN in V1 was severely undertrained (MAE $469.53 \pm 58.34$ MW), creating an illusion of massive gating gains simply because the gating network learned to discard the broken CNN.
* **Methodological Correction**:
  - Phase 5 strictly restores the original **LSTM + TCN + CNN** expert family.
  - No PatchTemporal, GRU, or Transformer modules are permitted in the canonical CAEG-Net architecture.
  - V2 and V3 artifacts are archived as exploratory research; they do not redefine or contaminate the canonical model.
  - The undertrained CNN issue is resolved methodologically through stabilized training (gradient clipping at 1.0, AdamW weight decay, and learning rate scheduling), creating an honest and rigorous benchmark.

### 2.2 Recent Baseline Error: Causality & Decoupling
* **Audited Concern**: Did the "recent baseline error" feature leak future load values $y_{t+1:t+24}$ into the context input at time $t$?
* **Formal Proof of Causality**:
  - Let $t$ be the forecast origin. The model predicts the horizon $\mathcal{H}_t = \{y_{t+1}, y_{t+2}, \dots, y_{t+24}\}$.
  - The recent baseline error feature is generated by an independent linear model (expanding walk-forward Ridge regression).
  - The Ridge baseline is fitted on history $\tau \le t - 24$. At time $t - 24$, it produces forecasts $\hat{y}_{t-23:t}^{\text{Ridge}}$.
  - When the clock reaches $t$, the realization $y_{t-23:t}$ has just been fully observed. The baseline error is calculated as:
    $$\mathcal{E}_t^{\text{recent}} = \frac{1}{24} \sum_{k=0}^{23} |\hat{y}_{t-k}^{\text{Ridge}} - y_{t-k}|$$
  - At origin $t$, every value in $y_{t-23:t}$ is strictly in the past ($\le t$). None of the future values $y_{t+1:t+24}$ are accessed or referenced.
  - **Verdict**: Causality is strictly preserved. There is zero target leakage into the future horizon.

### 2.3 Statistical Protocol: Overlapping Forecast Pathologies
* **Identified Flaw**: Historical evaluations computed Diebold-Mariano and paired $t$-tests across 1,318 contiguous hourly rolling windows with horizon $H=24$.
  - Two rolling forecasts starting at $t$ and $t+1$ overlap for 23 hours.
  - Their forecast errors are naturally autocorrelated up to lag $H-1 = 23$.
  - Treating these 1,318 hourly windows as independent samples inflated effective sample size, collapsed standard errors, produced extreme $t$-statistics ($|t| > 15$), and generated severe conflicts between test metrics.
* **Methodological Correction**:
  - **Non-Overlapping Daily Block Sampling**: The 1,318-hour test set is partitioned into $K=53$ non-overlapping 24-hour daily blocks ($53 \times 24 = 1,272$ hours).
  - Paired comparisons are evaluated on daily mean absolute errors:
    $$\bar{e}_{k}^{(m)} = \frac{1}{24} \sum_{h=1}^{24} |y_{24(k-1)+h} - \hat{y}_{24(k-1)+h}^{(m)}|$$
  - **Harvey-Leybourne-Newbold (HLN) Correction**: Multi-step Diebold-Mariano tests incorporate the HLN finite-sample correction factor:
    $$\text{HLN} = \sqrt{\frac{T + 1 - 2H + H(H-1)/T}{T}} \cdot \text{DM}$$
  - **Holm-Bonferroni FWER Control**: Multi-model pairwise hypothesis tests are adjusted using Holm-Bonferroni step-down correction.

### 2.4 Test-Set Contamination: Validation-Only Selection
* **Identified Flaw**: In prior iterations, hyperparameters, routing mechanisms, and loss weights were repeatedly adjusted based on test set feedback.
* **Methodological Correction**:
  - A strict **Validation-Only Selection Protocol** is enforced.
  - All ablation screening, hyperparameter tuning ($\rho$, $\lambda_{\text{aux}}$, $\beta_{\text{ent}}$), and routing choices are decided exclusively on the validation partition (Seed 42).
  - The test set is evaluated only once for the final candidate across the 5 canonical seeds (`[42, 123, 2024, 3407, 999]`).
