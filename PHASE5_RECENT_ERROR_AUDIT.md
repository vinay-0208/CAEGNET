## 1. Terminology Clarification & Conceptual Scope

In the original CAEG-Net documentation, the fourth context feature $C_{t, 4}$ was frequently referred to as "closed-loop error feedback" or "CAEG feedback".

### Rigorous Clarification:
1. **Source of the Signal**: The signal is generated by an **independent multi-step Ridge regression baseline** ($\alpha=100.0$) trained with an expanding-window chronological walk-forward protocol over historical load observations.
2. **Not Self-Feedback**: It is **NOT** the previous error of CAEG-Net itself (which would constitute autoregressive self-feedback).
3. **Correct Scientific Description**: It is an **external recent baseline forecast error** or **recent forecast difficulty indicator**. It informs the router whether a linear autoregressive forecaster experienced severe disruption over the preceding 24 hours.

## 2. Temporal Causality Audit

At forecast origin $t$, CAEG-Net generates a forecast for the horizon $[t+1 : t+24]$.

### 2.1 Information Timeline & Indexing
- At origin $t$, the most recently completed 24-hour day-ahead forecast concluded at time $t$.
- This forecast was launched at origin $s = t - 24$ to predict horizons $[s+1 : s+24] \equiv [t-23 : t]$.
- At time $t$, true observations $y[t-23 : t]$ have just become available.
- Therefore, the recent baseline MAE is defined as:
  $$\text{Recent\_Error}_t = \frac{1}{24} \sum_{h=1}^{24} \left| \hat{y}_{\text{Ridge}}[t - 24 + h] - y[t - 24 + h] \right|$$

### 2.2 Boundary Handoff Protocols
1. **Initial Warmup Period ($t < 524$)**:
   Before $t = 500 + 24$, insufficient out-of-sample forecast cycles exist to compute empirical error. The feature is initialized to the neutral prior $\mu_{\text{prior}} = 0.35$ (derived from the first out-of-sample cohort). Zero future targets are consulted.
2. **Train $\to$ Validation Boundary ($t_{\text{val}} < 24$)**:
   Validation origins $t < 24$ receive the completed out-of-sample forecast launched at late training origin $s = N_{\text{train}} - 24 + t$, evaluated against actual late training observations.
3. **Validation $\to$ Test Boundary ($t_{\text{test}} < 24$)**:
   Test origins $t < 24$ receive the completed forecast launched at late validation origin $s = N_{\text{val}} - 24 + t$, evaluated against actual late validation observations.

### 2.3 Verdict on Target Leakage
- **Target Horizons $y[t+1 : t+24]$**: **ZERO LEAKAGE**. The recent error window $[t-23 : t]$ ends strictly before the forecast horizon begins ($t+1$).
- **Lookback Independence**: Operates strictly on historical observations available at or before $t$.

## 3. Empirical Utility: A Controlled Research Question

While recent error is causally sound, its empirical benefit must be tested rather than assumed:
- In V1 (historical results), full CAEG-Net achieved $251.44 \pm 9.74\text{ MW}$, whereas CAEG-Net without recent error achieved $254.55 \pm 16.10\text{ MW}$ (a $+3.11\text{ MW}$ benefit).
- In V2/V3 experiments, recent baseline error exhibited lower signal-to-noise ratio in calm regimes.

### Phase 5 Testing Mandate:
A controlled ablation (Context with vs. without recent error) will be conducted on **Validation MAE** before locking the final Phase 5 candidate.
